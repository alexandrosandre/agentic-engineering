# Spanish Copy

Goal: Spanish that reads as written natively by a careful professional, in Spain Spanish (tuteo/vosotros, no LatAm variants), held consistently. You know RAE/Fundéu; the failure is defaulting to English habits and generated-text cadence instead of applying it. Watch for:

- Imported typography — the spaced dash as a pause (use paired rayas, colon, or parentheses), English quotes and casing, decimal points, unspaced %.
- Cadence and calques — chained «no es X, es Y», triads of examples, filler openers and closers, connector chains carrying no argument; «eventualmente», «en base a», «soportar», gerundio de posterioridad, ser-passives where Spanish uses the reflexive.
- Translated dev jargon — Spanish for developers keeps the English terms the team says out loud (merge, PR, draft, nits, repo, red/green TDD, worktree, preview, a workflow's phase and role names): «fusionar», «base de código» or «Construir» for Build read as wrong, not as careful.
- Formal verbs on slides — prefer plain ones («asignar», «validar») over «convocar», «ratificar».

Distilled from RAE/Fundéu and documented frontier-model tics; re-vet on model generation turnover. The dev-jargon and slide-verb lines are the team's own corrections, not model tics: keep them through that re-vet.
